# Harbour North RC3 Beta Test Checklist

Record **Pass**, **Fail**, or **Needs review** beside each item.

## 1. Clean start

- Open the app with no existing Harbour North data.
- Confirm the welcome and guided setup are understandable.
- Add both partners.
- Confirm single-person and couple modes work.
- Confirm every required field has a useful error message.

## 2. Actual household plan

- Import the latest Ryan and Liz backup.
- Confirm both partners appear correctly.
- Confirm retirement ages, incomes, pensions, CPP and accounts.
- Compare account totals against the backup.
- Confirm no duplicate records are created.

## 3. Projection calculations

- Confirm starting investment balances.
- Confirm contributions before retirement.
- Confirm withdrawals reduce the correct accounts.
- Confirm TFSA contributions increase TFSA balances.
- Confirm pension income appears in single and couple plans.
- Confirm age-71 registered assets include only RRSP/RRIF accounts.
- Confirm ending portfolio plus other assets minus debt equals ending estate.
- Compare future-dollar and today's-dollar values.

## 4. Tax and strategy

For every main strategy:

- Confirm RRSP/RRIF withdrawals are taxable.
- Confirm TFSA withdrawals are not taxable.
- Confirm federal and provincial tax are populated.
- Confirm CPP and OAS income are included at the correct ages.
- Confirm OAS recovery tax appears where applicable.
- Confirm lifetime tax reconciles with annual tax.
- Review the RRSP/RRIF drawdown strategy in detail.
- Confirm Strategy and Results pages use the same ending-estate value.

## 5. Backup and recovery

- Create a backup.
- Make a plan change.
- Confirm the app shows that changes exist since backup.
- Refresh or leave and confirm the backup warning.
- Import the new backup.
- Import at least one older backup.
- Confirm Reset requires deliberate confirmation.
- Confirm the app remains usable after a failed import.

## 6. Reports and PDF

- Generate the professional report.
- Confirm Ryan and Liz appear correctly.
- Confirm the Harbour North logo is prominent on the cover.
- Confirm the Harbour North header repeats on printed/PDF pages.
- Confirm today's-dollar labels are clear.
- Confirm charts are not clipped.
- Save as PDF in Safari.
- Save as PDF in Chrome.
- Print at Letter size and review page breaks.

## 7. Devices and accessibility

- Desktop Safari
- Desktop Chrome
- iPad portrait
- iPad landscape
- Phone portrait
- Keyboard navigation
- Text zoom at 125% and 150%
- Button contrast and readable font colours

## 8. Release decision

RC3 Beta 1 can advance when:

- no calculation-critical failures remain
- no data-loss or import-critical failures remain
- all core workflows pass
- reports print professionally
- known non-critical defects are documented
