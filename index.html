      How benefit amounts are handled

      • Enter CPP as the estimated monthly amount payable at age 65. Harbour North stores the annual equivalent internally and adjusts it when the optimizer tests ages 60 or 70.
      • Enter OAS as the estimated monthly amount payable at age 65. Harbour North stores the annual equivalent internally and applies the OAS deferral adjustment when testing age 70.
      • Enter workplace pensions as monthly amounts. Harbour North stores the annual equivalent internally and treats it as taxable recurring income from its selected start age through its end age.
      • All records remain visible and editable in the Financial Ledger.

      How tax-bracket optimization works

      The model targets the indexed ceiling of the first federal tax bracket for each retired account owner. Available RRSP or RRIF funds may be drawn during low-income years, taxes are recalculated, required spending is funded, and after-tax excess is retained in cash. The model does not currently optimize individual capital-gain lots, pension-income splitting or explicitly calculate OAS recovery tax.
