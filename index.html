Privacy and control

Your financial plan belongs to you.

Harbour North is being designed with a privacy-first approach. Users should understand where their information is stored, how it is used, and how to back it up.

Before commercial launch, the product will include clear privacy documentation, secure data-handling standards, and straightforward backup and recovery guidance.

Frequently asked questions

Built to be clear from the beginning.

Who is Harbour North for?

It is intended for Canadians who want to understand retirement timing, income, investments, government benefits, withdrawal choices, and long-term sustainability.

Does it replace a financial planner?

No. Harbour North is an educational and planning tool. It can help you understand options and prepare for more informed discussions, but it does not provide individualized financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

Can couples build one plan?

Yes. The product is being designed to model household retirement decisions while still keeping each person's income, accounts, benefits, and retirement dates distinct.

Will the calculations be transparent?

Yes. A core product principle is that important outputs should be explainable and supported by clear assumptions and audit information.

When will Harbour North be available?

The product is currently moving through calculation validation and private beta testing. Beta access will expand as testing progresses.
