# Harbour North Brand Personality v1.0

## Brand idea

**A steady guide for the next stage of life.**

Harbour North should feel like a calm, capable planner sitting beside the user. It does not pressure, dramatize, or hide behind financial jargon. It explains the situation, shows the trade-offs, and helps the user move forward with confidence.

## Personality traits

### Calm
The product lowers anxiety. It uses measured language, spacious design, and clear hierarchy.

### Capable
Harbour North feels rigorous and dependable. Important numbers are traceable, assumptions are visible, and recommendations are supported by reasoning.

### Independent
The product does not sell investments, insurance, or financial products. Its role is to help users understand choices.

### Plain-spoken
It translates retirement planning into language ordinary Canadians can use without making the subject feel simplistic.

### Optimistic, not promotional
Harbour North focuses on possibility and preparedness. It avoids exaggerated promises, urgency, and fear.

### Canadian
The tone is practical, grounded, respectful, and built around Canadian retirement realities.

## Voice principles

1. Lead with the answer.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Show the trade-off.
4. Suggest the next useful action.
5. Keep important caveats visible.
6. Never use fear to drive conversion.

## Examples

**Avoid:** Your retirement plan failed.

**Use:** Your current assumptions create a projected shortfall beginning at age 82.

**Avoid:** Maximize your wealth now!

**Use:** Compare strategies that may improve after-tax income and preserve flexibility.

**Avoid:** Error.

**Use:** We could not complete this calculation. Review the highlighted information and try again.

**Avoid:** Buy Premium to unlock your financial future.

**Use:** Premium tools add deeper scenario analysis, strategy comparisons, and professional reports.

## Signature phrases

- Retire with confidence.
- See the impact before making the decision.
- Your numbers, made understandable.
- A clearer view of what comes next.
- Independent Canadian retirement planning.
- Understand the plan. Explore the options. Decide with confidence.

## Visual personality

- Deep harbour navy for trust and stability
- Sea-glass green for progress and optimism
- Warm off-white backgrounds instead of sterile grey
- Strong typography and generous whitespace
- Minimal decoration
- Photography focused on real Canadian landscapes, water, coastlines, mountains, docks, and quiet moments
- Avoid staged corporate handshakes, luxury clichés, or images that imply every retirement must look the same
