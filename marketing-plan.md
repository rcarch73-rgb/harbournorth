# Harbour North Marketing Plan v1.0

## Objective

Build trust with Canadian pre-retirees, convert qualified visitors into beta users, and develop a repeatable path from education to product adoption.

## Primary audience

Canadian households roughly 45–70 who are asking:

- Can we retire when we want?
- How much can we spend?
- When should we start CPP and OAS?
- Which accounts should we draw from first?
- How much tax might we pay?
- What happens if returns are lower than expected?
- Will we leave an estate?

## Positioning

**Harbour North is independent Canadian retirement planning software that turns complex projections into clear, understandable decisions.**

It is not positioned as an investment platform, robo-advisor, or replacement for individualized professional advice.

## Core campaign message

**Retire with confidence.**

Supporting message:

Harbour North helps Canadians understand retirement timing, income, taxes, withdrawal strategies, and long-term sustainability before making important decisions.

## Launch funnel

### 1. Discover
Channels:
- Educational retirement articles
- Search
- Referrals
- Canadian personal-finance communities
- Beta-user recommendations
- Advisor and accountant introductions

### 2. Understand
Assets:
- Landing page
- Three-minute product demo
- Sample retirement report
- “How Harbour North works” article
- Clear FAQ and privacy explanation

### 3. Trust
Proof:
- Transparent assumptions
- Calculation methodology
- Beta testimonials
- Sample scenarios
- Professional report examples
- Clear limitations and disclaimers

### 4. Try
Offer:
- Private beta
- Guided sample plan
- One completed household plan at no charge
- No credit card during initial beta

### 5. Convert
Premium value:
- Unlimited plans and scenarios
- Advanced tax and withdrawal comparisons
- Monte Carlo analysis
- Professional reports
- Annual review workflow

## Initial content pillars

1. **Retirement timing**
   - How much do I need to retire in Canada?
   - Can I retire at 55?
   - What changes if one spouse retires earlier?

2. **Government benefits**
   - When should I take CPP?
   - How delaying CPP changes lifetime income
   - OAS and the recovery tax

3. **Withdrawal planning**
   - RRSP, TFSA, or non-registered first?
   - Avoiding a large RRIF later
   - Managing taxable income after retirement

4. **Risk**
   - Sequence-of-returns risk
   - Inflation in retirement
   - Planning for longer life
   - What a Monte Carlo result actually means

5. **Planning clarity**
   - Today's dollars versus future dollars
   - Why retirement calculators disagree
   - Which assumptions matter most

## Beta launch sequence

### Week 1
- Publish landing page
- Connect beta form
- Invite 10–20 trusted testers
- Record baseline conversion and completion rates

### Week 2
- Publish sample report
- Release short walkthrough video
- Begin structured user interviews

### Week 3
- Publish first three educational articles
- Add real beta feedback to FAQ and onboarding
- Identify the most common point of confusion

### Week 4
- Refine positioning and homepage copy
- Add two or three approved testimonials
- Open the next beta cohort

## Email sequence

1. **Welcome:** What Harbour North is and what to expect
2. **Prepare:** Information to gather before building a plan
3. **Complete:** A reminder to finish the first projection
4. **Understand:** How to read the retirement outlook
5. **Explore:** Try one meaningful scenario comparison
6. **Feedback:** Ask what was clear, confusing, or missing

## Early metrics

- Landing-page beta conversion
- Percentage who begin a plan
- Percentage who complete a plan
- Time to first completed result
- Report-generation rate
- Return rate within 30 days
- Number of support questions per completed plan
- Referral rate
- Willingness to pay

## Marketing guardrails

- Do not promise a specific retirement outcome.
- Do not describe projections as guarantees.
- Do not imply individualized financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.
- Do not use fear, scarcity, or exaggerated urgency.
- Do not claim Harbour North is the most trusted or most accurate without evidence.
- Always distinguish product goals from established facts.
